Stochastic Heat Equation

The stochastic heat equation is the classical heat equation combined with a space-time white noise. Although it only adds a drift term in the system. The behaviour of the classical solution and stochastic solution are very different.

Classical heat equation (diffusion equation) has a nice smoothness property (the orange curve above). For example, although the temperature is like a step shape at time 0. The temperature still becomes smooth immediately after time 0.

However, with the (space-time white) noise, the temperature is no longer smooth (the blue curve). The solution is not even differentible. In fact, it is in the H{\"o}lder space with any exponent less than 1/2.
